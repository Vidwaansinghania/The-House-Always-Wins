Sources

Reading the numbers

Amounts are ranges such as $1,001 to $15,000. Totals add up the low ends and the high ends separately. Trade dates run __TRADE_SPAN__. Paper filings, which are scanned images, are not included.

What this is not

A disclosure shows that a trade happened and was reported. It says nothing about why, and it is not evidence of wrongdoing. Many of these accounts are run by advisers.
